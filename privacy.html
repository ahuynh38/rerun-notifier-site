Privacy policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

What it accesses

Rerun Notifier asks for read-only access to YouTube (the youtube.readonly scope). It reads only the signed-in user's own channel ID, list of uploads and each upload's privacy status. It cannot upload, edit or delete anything.

How the data is used

It uses that information only to decide when to post a video link to the user's own Discord channel. When a video becomes unlisted, the video's link is sent to a Discord webhook that the user configured.

Storage and sharing

The sign-in token and the list of already-announced video IDs are stored only on the user's own computer. No data is sent to the developer or to any other party, and nothing is sold or used for advertising.

Revoking access

You can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.
